Tomasz Bielaczyc

Mathematics was one of my early interests, particularly probability theory and chaos theory. In recent years, I've also become interested in data analysis and visualization, especially finding patterns and making sense of data.
I studied and worked at the University of Silesia for several years. During the pandemic, I created a website tracking the global spread of COVID-19 through charts and maps, and later analyzed data from industrial systems at ABB Motion. More recently, I've been experimenting with building interactive games and applications.
